# Voiceover: cue sheet

Speak along with `renders/kbc-adapt-teleprompter.mp4`. Each line appears on screen at the moment
you should start saying it; the blue bar shows how long you have.

## How to record (easiest)

Record **one audio file per scene** (7 files), e.g. with your phone's voice recorder or QuickTime:
`scene1.m4a` … `scene7.m4a`. Start the file, wait a second, read the scene's lines at the pace
of the teleprompter, stop. Leading silence is fine; I trim it and place each file at its scene start.
Each scene must fit its time slot (see below). Then send me the 7 files and I mix them under the clean video.

Tips: quiet room, phone ~20 cm from your mouth, rather a bit slower than too fast.
